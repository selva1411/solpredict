# Portfolio Page Override Specs

## Layout Design
*   **Ledger Rows:** Individual ledger records detailing YES/NO share holdings, amount spent, current status (Active, Settled, Cancelled), and calculated P&L.
*   **Action Badges:** Segmented withdrew, claim, or refund buttons styled using mechanical theme palettes.

## Custom Features
*   **Category Filter comparison:** Comparison selectors allowing traders to isolate and examine their prediction metrics (invested, P&L, win rates) on a per-category basis (e.g. Crypto, Tech, Sports).
